Where We Started

Born out of a frustrating apartment hunt in Maitama

In 2022, our founder spent three months searching for a two-bedroom apartment near the diplomatic zone. He visited dozens of properties. Half were already taken. A quarter didn't match the descriptions. A few were outright scams.

He wasn't alone. Everyone in Abuja had the same story. So he built the thing he wished existed — a platform where listings are real, owners are verified, and renters can filter for exactly what they need without the runaround.

Today, Abuja Rentals connects thousands of tenants with landlords and agents across the FCT, from student housing near UNIABUJA to executive properties in Asokoro and Wuse II.
