Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know about finding, renting, or listing a property on Abuja Rentals.

For Renters

Yes, completely free. Browsing listings, saving properties, making requests, and booking viewings costs nothing. We're paid by agents and landlords — not by renters.

Every listing on Abuja Rentals is reviewed before going live. Agents and landlords must verify their identity. If you ever spot something suspicious, use the report button on any listing and we'll investigate within 24 hours.

Absolutely. Use the "Make a Request" button to tell us exactly what you're looking for — location, budget, size, amenities. Our agents will send you matching properties directly to your dashboard.

Open any property listing and click "Book a Viewing". Pick your preferred date and time, leave any notes for the owner, and submit. You'll see the booking status update in your dashboard, and the owner will confirm or suggest a new time.

You can book a viewing, save it to your list, or contact the agent directly via WhatsApp if provided. Once you've inspected and agreed to terms with the landlord, the lease is handled directly between you — we don't take a cut of rent.

For Agents & Landlords

Create an account and apply to become an agent from your dashboard. Once approved (usually within 1–2 business days), you can list unlimited properties. We review each listing before it goes live to maintain quality.

We aim to review all applications within 1–2 working days. You'll get an email as soon as a decision is made. If approved, you'll gain immediate access to agent features including client request matching and property listing tools.

You can only respond to client requests with your own listed properties. This keeps accountability clear and ensures the renter is dealing with a verified listing you actually manage.

We're currently free for agents in our early access phase. Premium features — like featured placement and priority matching — will be introduced later. You'll always get advance notice before anything changes.

Account & Privacy

Never. Your personal information is used only to operate your account and connect you with relevant properties. We don't sell it to third parties, period. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Go to Settings in your dashboard and select "Delete Account". Your data will be permanently removed within 30 days. If you're an agent with active listings, those will be unpublished first.

Click "Forgot password" on the login page. We'll send a reset link to your registered email address. If you don't see it in a few minutes, check your spam folder or email us at support@abujarentals.com.
